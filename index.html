OpeningDrill Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

OpeningDrill does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device with no network requests, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage data is collected.
  • No data is shared with third parties.
  • No cookies or identifiers are used.

All opening data and chess engine analysis runs locally on your device.
